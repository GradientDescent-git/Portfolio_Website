Finemed PharmaAI — Demand Intelligence Platform

Finemed PharmaAI

Pharmaceutical Demand Intelligence and Machine Learning Forecasting Platform

✓ Production-Oriented Architecture

The Business Problem

Pharmaceutical operations must work with complex historical ERP exports and medicine demand patterns that can be highly intermittent. Reliable forecasting requires structured data processing, robust validation, and forecasting strategies appropriate for different demand behaviours.

System Architecture

  • Hybrid Forecasting: Classical intermittent-demand forecasting with TSB and foundation-model forecasting using Chronos-2.
  • Validation-Based Model Selection: Forecasting models are evaluated using historical backtesting rather than selected solely by assumption.
  • Medallion Data Pipeline: Raw ERP exports are processed through structured data layers for downstream analytics and forecasting.
  • Grounded AI Workflow: Natural-language intent can be separated from deterministic data retrieval to reduce unsupported numerical responses.

Platform Capabilities

  • ERP Data Processing: Structured ingestion and validation of historical pharmaceutical data exports.
  • Demand Forecasting: Medicine-level forecasting with backtesting, diagnostics, and model comparison.
  • Operational Interface: A multi-tab interface for forecasts, demand analysis, data operations, and assistant workflows.
  • API-Oriented Architecture: Designed around modular services and structured application components.
